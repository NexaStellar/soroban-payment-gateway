# Code of Conduct

We expect participants to treat one another with respect and to keep project
spaces welcoming, constructive, and focused on the work.

Examples of expected behavior include listening to different viewpoints,
offering actionable feedback, and taking responsibility for mistakes.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces where someone is representing the project. Maintainers may edit or
remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

To report a concern, contact a repository maintainer privately through GitHub.
Include relevant links and a concise description. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, and retaliation for a good-faith report is not
acceptable.
